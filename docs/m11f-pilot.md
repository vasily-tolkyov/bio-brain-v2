# M11f phase 1 — stopped at the balance preflight

**INCOMPLETE / FAIL.** No constants or action threshold are proposed as accepted. The full seed-7 world pilot and the three-seed acceptance were not run. Stopped under the instruction to report numbers rather than add a mechanism or redefine a check.

The strict measured `u*(0)=0` requirement conflicts with continuing N-only creation from the comparator's nonzero rest background. A deleted connection is not an absorbing state: background N can recreate it while its cause occupies a past line. This is already allowed by M11e's rest-tail creation policy. Changing eta_S/eta_M does not remove that source of positive weight; pruning below the measured detection floor cannot remove a newborn above the floor either. An exact-zero population mean and a per-generation extinction requirement are different checks. No substitution between them has been made here.

Mean and population SD use the last 100 event endpoints, with absence counted as zero. Each probability restarts the event and comparator RNGs at seed 7. The isolated fixture exposes one candidate A@4→B connection and its active past line at the expected target onset. It runs the actual `SpikingErrors` and `ThreeFactor`, including N-only recreation and comparator-silent misses. It does not run the sensory or layer-2 attractor networks. The existing workspace's partial timing implementation was used: linear, symmetric credit with activation at arrival and zero at ±500 ms; miss credit is activation. Its inherited trial constants are eta_S=0.205, eta_M=0.24, g_floor=0.2, boost=0.05, tau_boost=5000 ms, w_init=0.01, w_max=0.1. Detection-floor pruning and the action gate are not yet implemented. With settled boost=1, S=0.205 and M=0.24, the requested analytic equation is

`p*S*(1-u) = (1-p)*M*(1-0.8*u)`.

Writing x=pS and y=(1−p)M, its stable clipped solution is zero for x≤y, otherwise `(x-y)/(x-0.8*y)`. This equation omits the birth term generated by background N. The measured curve is monotone but fails the maximum adjacent jump: 0.5→0.67 rises by **0.503988**, exceeding 0.4. It also fails exact zero at p=0. These inherited constants are rejected; no replacement constants were selected. This is the only completed real-comparator candidate curve. Preliminary scalar recurrences were not accepted as real-learner evidence.

### Exact zero-hit counterexample

[Independent deterministic audit](../out/m11f-pilot/zero-audit.json), [audit log](../out/m11f-pilot/zero-audit.log). Repeating the p=0 experiment reproduced the same mean and SD and retained each actual weight-changing window.

| 2 | 5300 | 0 | 0.1 | 0 / −1.5 | 18 / 16.5 | Initial connection deleted |

At birth, `Neff=4.5−3*sqrt(1.5)=0.8257653858>0`. There is no prediction to generate a miss against the new generation at that window. The recreated connection lives 4500 ms and occupies one of the final 100 event endpoints: mean `0.1/100=0.001`, SD `0.00994987437`. There are no target onsets, no STDP hits, and no delayed matched-onset ambiguity. All three predicted misses are recorded; two clear the comparator deadband. The final connection state is absent, but the specified mean is not zero.

Suppressing this N birth, deleting it immediately despite u_init>u_floor, excluding it from the average, or relabeling the final absent state as a zero last-100 mean would change a mechanism or a check. None was done. The supervisor must resolve how the exact-zero settled-strength criterion coexists with authorized background N recreation before this experiment can select passing constants.

[Measurement script](../experiments/m11f-floor.mjs), [raw data](../out/m11f-pilot/floor.json), [log](../out/m11f-pilot/floor.log). The floor script was already present at task entry and was run without source changes.

The first sampled mean crossing brackets the floor between 0.015 and 0.02. Linear interpolation gives

`u_floor = 0.015 + (3*sqrt(1.5) − 3.03) * (0.02 − 0.015) / (3.72 − 3.03) = 0.019668366769382366`.

Thus `w_init/w_max=0.1 > u_floor`. This is a mean-crossing estimate, not a claim that individual trials below it never detect misses; the detection rates show the distinction explicitly. No floor constant was installed in the runtime after the stop.
